## Verification and rollback

The repository gate pins the exact adoption record, verifies its protected
documents and compares the complete catalog transition. Its result is a list of
failures, never a token, verified planning input or permission. Before reading
document paths it snapshots the exact serialized evidence that matched the pin.
An independent reviewer still establishes owner provenance and technical scope.

Root checks, API/schema fixtures, SDK gates, mutation cases, strict protocol
resolution and role-separated promotion review are required on the final
immutable target. Current preparation records are not substitutes for those
future results. Historical rejected candidates and their originals remain
preserved by the controller, not silently rewritten as approvals.

Before any implementation, rollback withdraws the adoption and dependent pins
through another reviewed change. It deletes no product data: this package creates
none. Later product work needs its own qualified migration, API/E2E/coverage,
authority, retention and restoration evidence before activation.
