# P02 v1 contracts: rule set v3, whole-organization export, excerpt, usage statement

Decision identifier: `urn:libre-ai:decision:2026-10-22-p02-v1-contracts`.
Status: proposed. The four entries are `candidate`, not `locked`; their names are
provisional until the LEXICON entry of `libre-ai/project-governance` is signed.
Arbitration reference: 2026-10-22, the owner's design decisions D1 to D11 of the
P02 v1 design record (contract list in its section 4, names left to the authoring
agent in its section 9). The owner's signature on the contract creation itself is
still to be given before merge.

## Context

The P02 information watch (`libre-ai/information-watch`, provisional) needs four
contracts that this authority does not carry:

- a rule language that expresses AND, OR, NOT over content, URL, language,
  categories and dates, with actions to hide, keep, tag, mark read, follow, put
  to read and alert (product requirement FR-14);
- a complete, reimportable export of one organization, with the provenance of
  every item (G1) and a digest (G2, invariant I-10);
- an excerpt sent to the notes product P04 as a Markdown file with provenance;
- a usage statement, signed, counting and carrying no content (FR-34).

`curation-rule-set.v2` is locked: one condition per rule, two decisions, six
fields. It cannot express FR-14, and it cannot change in place.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Extend `curation-rule-set.v2` in place | Forbidden: a locked contract never changes in place |
| New `curation-rule-set.v3` major next to v2 | v2 stays consumable by its consumers; v3 is a new candidate |
| Extend `curated-item-export.v3` to carry the whole organization | Its shape is items only; widening it changes a candidate that is under review |
| New `p02-export.v1` for the whole organization | One document, one digest, one authority; the item shape is redefined with full provenance |
| Reuse a notes contract for the excerpt | No notes contract exists for Markdown with provenance |
| New `knowledge-excerpt.v1` | The excerpt is a projection of a JSON object; the Markdown file is its lossless form |
| New `usage-statement.v1` | Closed counters-only object, signed; nothing to reuse |

## Decision

Add the four contracts as `candidate` entries, appended after the registered
additions, in `contracts/catalog-post-lock-additions.v1.json` (pinned digest in
`tools/quality/specification-lock.ts`), each with a review dossier under
`docs/reviews/p02-v1-contracts-review.md`:

| Contract | Schema | Classification | Required independent reviews |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `curation-rule-set-v3` | `contracts/schemas/curation-rule-set.v3.schema.json` | tenant-private | architecture, security |
| `p02-export-v1` | `contracts/schemas/p02-export.v1.schema.json` | personal | architecture, security, privacy, cryptography |
| `knowledge-excerpt-v1` | `contracts/schemas/knowledge-excerpt.v1.schema.json` | personal | architecture, security, privacy |
| `usage-statement-v1` | `contracts/schemas/usage-statement.v1.schema.json` | tenant-private | architecture, security, privacy, cryptography |

Semantics: `contracts/<name>/SEMANTICS.md`. Vectors and schema fixtures:
`contracts/fixtures/<name>/`.

Two properties are not expressible in JSON Schema and are enforced by the test
oracle or by the consumer, and are recorded as such in the semantics: the
32-leaf budget of a rule (`curation-rule-set-v3`) and the cross-references of an
export. The export digest is the RFC 8785 oracle of `tools/quality/export-digest.ts`.

## Consequences

- This record admits catalog entries. It grants no runtime admission and is not a
  role verdict on any content; conformance is proved in the product repository,
  against this authority at a pinned revision.
- The entries stay `candidate` until each required role has produced its pass.
  Nothing here is locked.
- Merge waits for the owner's signature on the names and on the contract creation.
